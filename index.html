A paragraph is a group of sentences, usually focused on a single main idea, that works as a cohesive unit to organize and convey information within a larger text. It typically starts on a new line, sometimes with an indent, and functions as a way for readers to understand the structure and main points of the author's message. Effective paragraphs often include a topic sentence, supporting sentences with details or examples, and a concluding sentence that transitions to the next idea.
